Shepit widget: six concepts

Shepit is a dictation app for macOS: hold a key, speak Ukrainian or English, and clean text lands in the focused app. These are six directions for its floating widget. Each stage is a slice of the desktop with the widget at its default spot, bottom middle. Every concept plays a full dictation on loop; the buttons under a stage pin one state. Hover a widget to see its idle hint. The live words come from Parakeet while you speak (dim words may still change); the final text is Whisper plus Claude.

Choosing

  • Pick by number. Mixes work too, for example "4, with the waveform from 1".
  • All six fit a borderless NSPanel. Concepts with text above the widget (1, 3, 5) need a taller panel or a second one for the caption.
  • Colors: black glass at about 70% opacity with a 20 pt blur, white text, red only for the recording dot, amber for errors, green for success.
