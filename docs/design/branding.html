Shepit logo directions

Four marks for Shepit, a macOS dictation app built around a black glass widget. Each card shows the app icon, the logo with the name on dark and light, the menu bar glyph in both menu bar appearances, and small sizes for legibility. The icon background borrows the widget's glass and the teal and amber of its preview wallpaper.

Picking: reply with a number, or a mix such as "3 with the glow of 1". The chosen mark becomes the app icon in every size, the menu bar icon (a template image, so macOS tints it), and the logo in the welcome tour, What's new and About.
