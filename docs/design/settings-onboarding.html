Shepit settings and onboarding

Shepit is a dictation app for macOS: hold a key, speak Ukrainian or English, and clean text lands in the focused app. This page shows three pieces around the widget: the welcome tour on first launch, the Settings window, and the card that appears after an update that adds something new. Everything on the page is clickable.

Welcome tour

Five short steps on first launch. It is the same black glass as the widget, and each step does one thing.

Speak instead of typing

Hold Right ⌥, say what you want to write, let go. Shepit types it into the app you are in, in Ukrainian or English.

Two permissions

Speech recognition runs on this Mac. The audio never leaves it.

MicrophoneTo hear you while the key is held.
AccessibilityTo notice the key in any app and paste the text where your cursor is.

Pick a look

The widget sits at the bottom of the screen. Drag it anywhere and it stays there. You can switch later in Settings.

Polish the text

An AI agent you already use can fix punctuation, names and code terms. It gets the text only, never the audio.

No AI agent found on this Mac, so Shepit will insert the text as heard. Install Claude Code or Codex and it shows up here.

What's new

After an update that adds something you can see, a card rises from the widget once. Bug-fix releases stay quiet.
